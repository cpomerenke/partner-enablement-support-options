Best Practice

Ask well,
get answered fast.

01
Be specific
Name the module, the employee, the date range or the exact error. Vague questions trigger more clarifying questions.
02
Bring the evidence
Paste error messages. Attach screenshots. Mention what you've already tried. Cuts back-and-forth by half.
03
Answer the bot
Herobot's prompts route you to the right team. Skipping or one-wording them sends you down the wrong path.
04
Flag honestly
"Urgent" should mean work is genuinely blocked. Honest flagging helps us route real emergencies to the front of the queue, faster.
VAGUE — SLOWS YOU DOWN
"Payroll is broken."
SPECIFIC — GETS YOU A FIX
"Weekly pay run for the 6 Apr period is stuck on 'processing' for 4 employees — error E-1042. Have tried re-running."
